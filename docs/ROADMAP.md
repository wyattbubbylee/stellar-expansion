# Stellar Expansion roadmap

## Default gameplay design
- Dyson construction is the top logistics priority.
- Cargo dispatch defaults to automatic.
- High-detail nearby shuttles; far-away shipments simulated as records.
- Main controls are interactive 3D holograms instead of flat GUIs.

## Future
- Mega Launcher with cargo and arbitrary connected module scanning
- Construction drones that actually place blocks
- In-world 3D blueprint hologram controls
- Asteroid mining and multi-station shipping
- Aeronautics adapters for moving ships and ship-relative interactions
- Artificial gravity, curved walking surfaces and full Dyson rings

No gameplay milestone is complete until tested in-game.
